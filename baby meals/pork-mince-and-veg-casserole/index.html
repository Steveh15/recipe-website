    Pork Mince and Veg Casserole

    -[# Pork Mince & Vegetable Casserole with Herby Diced Potatoes

    Ingredients

    Pork & veg casserole

    • 400–500g pork mince
    • 1 tbsp olive oil
    • 1 small onion, finely chopped
    • 1 carrot, diced small
    • 1 courgette, diced small
    • 1 tbsp tomato purée
    • 1 tbsp plain flour
    • ~2 cups unsalted chicken or vegetable stock
    • ½ tsp dried oregano or thyme
    • Black pepper (very light)

    Herby potatoes

    • 3–4 medium potatoes, diced small
    • 1 tbsp olive oil
    • ½ tsp dried mixed herbs or oregano

    To finish (adults): - Salt
    - Extra pepper
    - Optional: splash of Worcestershire sauce or mustard

    Instructions

    1. Cook the potatoes
      Boil diced potatoes until soft.
      Drain and toss with olive oil and dried herbs.
      Set aside.

    2. Brown the pork mince
      Heat oil in a large pan or casserole dish.
      Add pork mince and cook gently until just coloured.

    3. Add vegetables
      Stir in onion, carrot, and courgette.
      Cook for 5–7 minutes until softened.

    4. Build the casserole
      Stir in tomato purée and flour.
      Cook for 1 minute, then gradually add stock, stirring well.
      Add herbs and a light pinch of pepper.

    5. Simmer
      Simmer gently for 15–20 minutes until veg is soft and sauce thickened but spoonable.

    6. Split for baby
      Spoon out baby portion before seasoning.
      Mash lightly or chop finely as needed.
      Serve with a small portion of herby potatoes.

    7. Finish for adults
      Season remaining casserole with salt and optional extras.
      Serve with the rest of the potatoes.

    Notes

    • Pork mince stays soft and moist — ideal for babies.
    • Sauce should be loose, not dry.
    • Potatoes can be mashed for baby if diced pieces are awkward.
    • Freezes very well.
